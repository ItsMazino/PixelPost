Photos are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). These are sample creative assets, not photos taken by the project author.

Fonts are self-hosted through Fontsource: Fraunces for the interface; DM Sans, DM Serif Display, Caveat, and Bebas Neue for postcard artwork. Font license files are distributed with their respective `@fontsource/*` packages (SIL Open Font License).

Interface icons come from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/license). Postcard stickers, postal graphics, grain, and the envelope favicon are drawn in the project. UI primitives are generated from [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com), built on Radix UI.

Visual research: [Papier](https://www.papier.com/) for editorial stationery presentation, and [Vacation](https://vacation.inc/) for retro leisure styling. PixelPost uses its own layout, wording, and artwork; brand assets from these references are not included.
